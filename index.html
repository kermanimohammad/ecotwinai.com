Capabilities

What the sandbox does today.

This is the live workspace at app.ecotwinai.com. In-app simulation runs are not open to every user yet — those are under Roadmap.

01 — Model

Model the built environment

A real-time 3D scene with a grid, lighting, soft shadows, and camera controls. Draw rectangles, circles, polygons, linear paths, and spheres. Add buildings — including generators and merge — plus trees, furniture, and terrain. Select, move, rotate, scale, and duplicate, with undo and redo. Edit meshes, snap to vertices, and measure, with an object list and properties panel beside the model.

02 — Surfaces

Assign surfaces analysis can use

The Types manager holds catalogs for plants, soils, water, roads, buildings, optical materials, carbon emission factors, PV parameters, and generic surfaces, aligned with CDT-UBEM-style data packages. Stack surface layers and assign materials so the twin is prepared for simulation, not only for a picture.

03 — Map

Start from a real place

Map Bridge lets you pick an area and load urban features into the scene. The default path brings buildings from OpenStreetMap and Overture, with heights where those sources provide them. Models can store georeferencing, and GeoJSON can be imported.

04 — Files

Keep the formats projects already use

.CDT (CityDigTwin) is the primary project package. .ecofile carries the scene and settings for simulation pipelines. Export meshes, or bring existing geometry and catalogs in.

  • .CDT
  • .ecofile
  • STL
  • OBJ
  • GLB
  • FBX
  • GeoJSON

05 — Access

Accounts and subscriptions

Register and sign in with email and password. Google and Microsoft sign-in are available when they are configured, along with password reset and email verification. Where guest mode is turned on, you can look around before subscribing. Plans run through Stripe Checkout. An active subscription keeps Save and Export available. How it works

Three steps from a block to a decision.

Model and surface assignment are in the app now. The third step is the simulation release currently rolling out.

  1. Step 1 · Available

    Model in the browser

    Draw the neighborhood, or pull buildings in from the map. Edit the massing until the scheme is the one you mean to test.

  2. Step 2 · Available

    Assign surfaces

    Give roofs, walls, ground, and landscape the properties an energy or microclimate study expects. Export a CDT or EcoFile when the model is ready.

  3. Step 3 · Rolling out

    Simulate and decide

    PALM microclimate and EnergyPlus runs, with results back in the scene, so a design change can be judged on performance. The Simulation menu is opening for subscribers shortly. It is not yet available to every user.

Questions

Plain answers about the company and the sandbox.

What is EcoTwin AI?

EcoTwin AI is a company that builds software for urban design and energy simulation. The work is for architects, planners, and engineers who need more sustainable cities, from early massing to a digital twin that is ready for performance analysis.

What is Eco Digital Twin Sandbox?

Eco Digital Twin Sandbox is the browser app at app.ecotwinai.com. It is one workspace for 3D urban modeling, surface and material assignment, map import, and project files. This website explains the company. It is not the editor.

Who is it for?

Architects, urban planners, energy and sustainability consultants, researchers, municipalities, and engineering teams working on buildings and neighborhoods.

What works in the app today?

You can model in 3D, assign surface types and layers, bring buildings in from OpenStreetMap and Overture through Map Bridge, and save or export .CDT, .ecofile, STL, OBJ, GLB, FBX, and GeoJSON. Accounts, sign-in, and Stripe subscriptions are live. Save and Export need an active plan.

Can I run microclimate or building energy simulation now?

Not as a general release. PALM microclimate, EnergyPlus building energy, in-scene results, and scenario comparison are in the stack and rolling out. The Simulation menu is not yet available to every user.

How do I contact EcoTwin AI?

How much does a subscription cost?
